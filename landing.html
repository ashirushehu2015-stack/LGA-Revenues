Common Questions

Citizen & Taxpayer Guidelines

Find quick answers to common questions about tax compliance, registration, and payments in Zamfara State.

How do I register my business for tax payments?
You can register your business by visiting the Payer Portal. Click on "New Business Registration", fill in your business details, upload required identifications, and submit. You will receive a unique Taxpayer ID (Payer Ref) via SMS and email within 24 hours.
What payment methods are supported?
We support highly secure digital payments via Paystack. You can pay using your debit card (Mastercard, Visa, Verve), bank transfer, USSD, or direct bank deposit. All online payments generate an instant, verifiable digital receipt with a unique transaction reference number.
How do I verify the authenticity of an invoice or tax officer?
Every official LGA RevMax invoice has a QR code and a unique Invoice Reference number. You can search this ID on our Payer Portal or support panel to verify its authenticity. Authentic LGA Tax Officers carry official digital ID cards containing QR codes that verify their credentials.
What should I do if I am overcharged or have a complaint?
Zamfara State operates a transparent grievance redressal system. You can file a formal complaint by visiting our Grievances Portal or expanding the citizen support widget in the bottom-right corner. All complaints are tracked with a Grievance ID and resolved within 3 working days.
