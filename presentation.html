LGA RevMax Overview

Revolutionizing Revenue

A Modern, Transparent, and Automated Management System for Local Government Areas in Zamfara State.

The Challenge

Traditional revenue collection faces significant hurdles that impede growth and transparency.

Manual Processes

Paper-based tracking leads to errors, delays, and lost records.

Revenue Leakage

Lack of real-time monitoring allows for significant financial discrepancies.

Poor Transparency

Difficult for citizens to verify payments and for administrators to audit records.

The RevMax Solution

An integrated ecosystem designed to bring efficiency and accountability to every step of the revenue cycle.

Automation

Digital assessment and billing eliminate manual bottlenecks.

Transparency

Real-time audit trails and instant digital receipts for every transaction.

Security

Role-based access control and secure payment gateway integration.

Core Modules

LGA RevMax is built with specialized modules for both administrators and citizens.

Admin Dashboard

Centralized control for user management, LGA configuration, and system settings.

Payer Portal

Self-service portal for business registration, tax assessment, and online payments.

Analytics Engine

Deep insights into collection trends, targets, and officer performance metrics.

Innovation at Core

Leveraging technology to solve complex administrative challenges.

Smart Tax Calculator

Dynamic assessment based on business type, location, and asset valuations.

Auto-Notifications

Automated SMS and Email reminders for upcoming tax deadlines and payments.

Digital Receipts

Instant generation of professional, verifiable receipts with QR code support.
